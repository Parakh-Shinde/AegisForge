## Purpose

This document converts the preserved v0.9 holdout-v2 failures into an explicit v0.10
engineering backlog. It does not revise the original result or claim improved performance.

The machine-readable registry is
[`evaluation/v0.10-error-analysis.json`](../evaluation/v0.10-error-analysis.json).
Its source of truth remains the immutable
[first-run report](../reports/holdout-v2-first-run.json).

## Dataset transition

Holdout-v2 remains valid historical evidence for v0.9. Once its failures guide v0.10
development, it is classified as an adapted-regression source and must not be used for a
new unbiased effectiveness claim.

A future independent measurement requires:

1. completing detector changes against tuning and adapted-regression data;
2. freezing the resulting detector and policy;
3. preparing a fresh holdout-v3 after the freeze;
4. executing holdout-v3 once; and
5. preserving its complete report and provenance.

## Change boundary

This analysis branch must not modify detector logic, the holdout-v2 corpus, the preserved
first-run report, or the v0.9 freeze. Remediation items describe testable objectives for
later feature branches; they are not evidence that those capabilities already exist.
